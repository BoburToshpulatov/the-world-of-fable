Privacy Policy

Lumi: A Journey Through Fables

We are committed to providing a safe and privacy-friendly experience for all users.

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

Introduction

Lumi: A Journey Through Fables is an educational storytelling and sequencing game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users interact with the app.

Information We Collect

Lumi: A Journey Through Fables does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information from users.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location data, photographs, contacts, payment information, or any other personal information through the app.

How the App Works

The app is designed to function without user registration or online profiles. Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on the user's device to support gameplay and improve the user experience.

User Privacy

Protecting user privacy is important to us. We are committed to minimizing data collection and providing a safe experience for all users.

Third-Party Services

Lumi: A Journey Through Fables does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, behavioral tracking tools, or external marketing platforms.

The app operates independently and does not share user information with third parties.

Children and Young Users

The app is intended for educational and family use. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children or other users.

If you believe personal information has been provided through the app, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to investigate and address the issue.

Retention and Deletion

Any game progress or preferences stored by the app remain on the user's device until deleted.

Users may remove locally stored data by resetting the app's saved data or uninstalling the app from their device.

Data Security

Because personal information is not collected by the app, privacy risks are minimized. Reasonable technical measures are applied to protect the integrity of locally stored game data.

International Transfers

The app does not transfer gameplay data to external servers. Any communication sent directly by email may be processed through the user's and recipient's email providers.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection regarding personal information voluntarily provided through direct communication such as email.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, legal requirements, or operational practices.

Any updates will be published on this page together with a revised "Last Updated" date.
