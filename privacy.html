Privacy Policy

Lumi: A journey through fables

A privacy-first educational game with no account creation and progress stored locally on the device.

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

Introduction

Lumi: A journey through fables is an educational storytelling and sequencing game for children and families. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users interact with the app.

Information We Collect

The app does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, contacts, or payment information.

Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for children and young users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided through the app, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to investigate.

Local Progress and Preferences

Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on the user’s device to support gameplay, accessibility settings, and language choices. This information is not sent to an external server by the app.

Third-Party Services

Lumi: A journey through fables does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, behavioral tracking tools, or external marketing platforms inside the app.

Data Security

Because personal information is not collected by the app, privacy risks are minimized. Reasonable care is taken to protect the integrity of locally stored game progress and preferences.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app, legal requirements, or operational practices. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app’s privacy practices, contact us at bobur.vocal@gmail.com.
