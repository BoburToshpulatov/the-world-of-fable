Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

Introduction

Lumi: A Journey Through Fables is a fairy-tale sequencing game designed to help users learn storytelling, sequencing, logic, and chronological order through interactive story activities. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to privacy.

Information We Collect

Lumi: A Journey Through Fables does not collect personal information from users. The app does not require users to create an account, provide a name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, or any other personal data.

User Privacy

Protecting user privacy is important to us. The app is designed to work without collecting, storing, or sharing personal information.

How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use personal information for analytics, advertising, tracking, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Lumi: A Journey Through Fables does not use third-party services, third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or external tracking tools inside the app.

Data Security

Since the app does not collect or store personal information, there is no personal user data stored by us. We aim to keep the app simple, safe, and privacy-friendly.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app’s privacy practices, contact us at bobur.vocal@gmail.com.
