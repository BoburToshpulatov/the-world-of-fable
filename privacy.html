Introduction

Fairy Tale Sequence is a children’s app designed to help kids learn storytelling, sequencing, logic, and chronological order through fairy-tale activities. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when families use the app.

Information We Collect

Fairy Tale Sequence is designed for children and does not require children to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as a child’s name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, or contacts.

If future versions include optional analytics or app performance tools, we will aim to collect only limited, non-personal information needed to improve app reliability and the play experience.

Children’s Privacy

Protecting children’s privacy is especially important to us. The app is intended for young users and is designed to avoid collecting personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove it if needed.

How We Use Information

If limited technical or usage information is collected in the future, it would be used to maintain the app, improve performance, fix issues, and understand which features are helpful to families. We do not sell children’s personal information.

Third-Party Services

The app may be distributed through Apple’s App Store and may use standard platform services provided by Apple. Any third-party services used by the app will be selected carefully and reviewed for suitability in a children’s experience.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect any information associated with the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we aim to minimize collection and reduce privacy risk wherever possible.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app’s privacy practices, contact us at bobur.vocal@gmail.com.
