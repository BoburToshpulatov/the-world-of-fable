Children at L'Arcobaleno school playing Lumi.

Designed for focused, joyful iPad play, Lumi turns sequencing into a hands-on story activity children can understand, repeat, and celebrate.

Game story

Lord Tempus has shattered the fairy tales.

Guided by Lumi, children explore an enchanted map, enter story islands, and rebuild classic fables chapter by chapter. Each level presents illustrated cards to drag into the right slots, turning story order into a playful puzzle.

When a scene feels tricky, players can flip a card to read a gentle clue. Completed chapters unlock the growing storybook and bring the magic back to the world of fables.
