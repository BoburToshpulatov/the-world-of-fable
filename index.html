Game concept

Every fairy tale becomes a sequence adventure.

Children choose a fairy tale, enter story missions, arrange illustrated cards in the correct order, and unlock the next part of the adventure. Each completed sequence helps them understand what happens first, what comes next, and how a story reaches its ending.

How it works

Four clear moments, designed for young players.

1

Choose a Tale

Kids begin on a friendly story map and select the fairy tale they want to play.

2

Start a Mission

The tale opens as a small mission with a clear goal and illustrated story clues.

3

Arrange the Cards

Children place scenes in the right order, practicing cause, effect, and memory.

4

Continue the Story

Correct sequences unlock the next moment with calm feedback and playful motion.
